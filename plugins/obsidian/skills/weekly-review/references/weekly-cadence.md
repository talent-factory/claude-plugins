# Weekly Review — Cadence & Tips

## Recommended Time

**Friday 16:00–17:00** is optimal:
- The week is complete; memories are fresh.
- The following week has not yet created urgency.
- Sufficient distance from the work allows honest reflection.

Alternatives: Friday evening, Sunday evening. Avoid Monday — too much distance.

## Reflection Prompts (when the section is blank)

If the user has not yet completed the Reflection section from the previous
week, remind them gently on the next invocation:

> "Last week's review (W<N>) has no reflection yet — worth adding a few lines?"

## What a Good Review Looks Like

A well-formed weekly review contains:
- Concrete deliverables ("PR #42 merged"), not vague activity ("worked on X").
- At least one thing that could have gone better.
- A realistic top-3 for the following week (not ten items).
- The Reflection section preserved intact when re-running the skill.

## Recognising Patterns

After four or more weekly reviews, patterns become visible:
- Which task types remain chronically open?
- Which repositories receive the most commits?
- Which meeting types produce decisions versus status updates?

These observations can feed directly into `/ideas` as additional context.
